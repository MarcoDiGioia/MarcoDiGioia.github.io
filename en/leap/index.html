Three parts, one console

Host, protocol and client designed to work together.

🌑

Singularity

Host · Windows and Linux, x86_64 and ARM64

The engine on your home PC: finds launchers and apps on its own, isolates them in a console environment, captures audio and video and injects input. An admin dashboard on the host authorises devices.

📡

WARP

Protocol · authenticated

A single protocol for LAN discovery, PIN pairing, audio and video streams, 250 Hz binary input and window state.

🌅

Horizon

Client · TV, mobile, desktop

The native app in your living room: auto-discovery, quick keypad for the TV remote, 4-digit PIN pairing, task switcher and hardware decoding. No browser version.

Finds what you have installed

Singularity detects launchers, emulators and media apps and puts them in your console.
